## Project Type: B2B Testimonial
## Subjects: 4 — Drew DeMartinis (Dir. Accounting/Finance/IT, lead voice), Jordan Heifetz (CEO), Henry Hong (COO), Vlad (Controller)

> The Project Type tag is used by future agents to filter for relevant reference examples.

### Project Summary
A ~2:44 B2B customer testimonial for ECI (Deacom ERP), the quality/traceability arc of a four-video
series cut from one interview set. Drew carries ~70% of the film; the other three voices enter at
deliberate single points. First project run end-to-end through the live-partner Edit Agent viewer
(rounds v1–v5 in one session, one FCPXML round, finished in FCP same week).

### Act Structure
**Quality Is the Whole Game** — identity → traceability thesis → demanding customers → "it required
a more robust system." (Need/aspiration only — see chronology rule.)
**The System Won't Let It Ship Wrong** — before-state contrast (restored in FCP, 9s) → "best of all
5 worlds" → two movements: traceability/visibility, then quality gates on top.
**Proof, Not Promises** — CEO standard → the 99.99%-vs-99.5% audit story (the single proof anchor,
85% survival) → best-brands close ending on the product name.

### What Worked Well
- **Act-level chronology as a hard rule** (born on this project, promoted to SKILL-edit.md): Act 1
  states need — no segment may show the product in use before the story adopts it.
- **Two-movement mechanism act**: traceability first, then "on top of that" quality controls, with
  Vlad's "quality can actually see inventory" as the hinge. Jeff requested it; the final preserved it.
- **Single proof anchor**: the audit story carried Act 3 alone; the planned zero-recall second beat
  (quote + title card) was dropped without loss.
- **Structure survival**: 15 of 17 emitted entries survived to the final with zero reorders —
  selection/ordering calls held; finishing was pure tightening (~30% off every entry).

### What Was Difficult
- First-pass calibration: Jeff rejected a pre-trimmed, one-act-at-a-time opening build — he needs
  the WHOLE film wide before any cutting ("longer cut so I can make sure the story is all there").
- Recall stakes: "If you've ever had to do a recall, you know the cost" read as implying prior
  recalls; all experience-implying failure framing came out.
- Live-partner logistics: rebuilds while Jeff edits race his unsaved state (one edit lost, recovered
  from the tweak log); two open viewer instances fight over viewer-state.json.

### Corrections Jeff Made
- Restored the before-state (quality on PDFs/Excel) the agent had removed — but at 9s of 44s.
- Dropped both assertion beats (CEO "we win on quality", "every customer is a partnership") while
  keeping every demonstration beat: show > tell under runtime pressure.
- Compressed the vendor-selection story to its punchline ("best of all 5 worlds", 24% survival).

### Cardinal Rule Status
PASS both rules. Verbatim verified at emit via editcuts_to_segments (2 mid-segment fidelity notes,
both harmless filler played wider). Coherence verified at each proposal; Jeff's chronology rule
added a new class of Rule-2 check (tense/product references vs act position).

### Rules That Emerged
1. **Whole-film-first wide build** — all acts on the table before refinement; breadth in entries AND scope.
2. **Adoption-chronology rule** (promoted to SKILL-edit.md 2026-07-24).
3. **No implied-prior-failure framing** in client-story stakes.
4. **Demonstration > assertion** when cutting for runtime.
5. **Carry one tight before-state voice into the mechanism act** — contrast reads better against
   picture than on paper.
